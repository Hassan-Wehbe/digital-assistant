The difference

  • A: “What is it?” is one connected bar with icons, like tabs. “Where does it go?” is one line showing the chosen space, and Change › opens a list with a search box. That's easier with many spaces, and pills are never side by side.
  • B: what it is shown as big tiles, and the space stays as pills under a clear SPACE heading. Fewer taps for the space, but more pills on screen.
  • Both: opened from inside a built-in space (＋ New place), neither shows. It just says “In 📍 Places” (already fixed on branch claude/builtin-space-note).
