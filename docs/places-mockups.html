How it works

  • A starts every new place with one box (from Home ＋ → 📍 New place, from Places → ＋, and the 📍 Place kind on New note). After the pick, the usual form follows with the name, address and kind filled in.
  • B keeps today’s form and adds a 🔎 Find it on the map button at the top. Fewer changes, but the main way is one button among many.
  • Suggestions appear after 3 letters and a short pause (about 4–5 lookups a place, about 1–1.4 cents; at most 100 lookups a day each, about 20 places).
  • Without the Google key, or after the day’s limit, the list says “Type the full street address” and the phone’s free map lookup finds it (as Type an address does today).
  • Google asks apps to show “powered by Google” under its suggestions.

The spot

  • The spot comes from the phone’s own map lookup of the address Google showed (free). Google’s terms let Wilma keep only Google’s id for the place, not its coordinates.

One list for every picker

  • Used by a task’s “Where is it done?”, My day’s “Where do you leave from?” and a home’s address.
  • The list opens straight away, without the old three-button menu: homes first, then Places (newest first), then places in a locked space never.
  • A place with an address but no spot is listed; picking it looks the address up (Is this it?) and offers to keep the spot. With neither, it says Add an address and opens the place.
  • Typing filters by name, address and kind; nothing found offers 🔎 Find a new place on the map (section 1), which saves it into Places too.
