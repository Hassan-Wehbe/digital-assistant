# Phone checklist for the build after versionCode 22 (most likely versionCode 23)

For the owner, on the Play build after versionCode 22. It carries your three changes to the
planners (`docs/planners-plan.md`, "Changes after versionCode 22"): the calendar is only planned
around, any day, and kinds of date. The server part goes live with the `chat` deploy before the
build. **Pro** is needed. About 20 minutes. Do versionCode 22's checklist first if you haven't
(`docs/versioncode22-phone-checklist.md`), skipping its calendar lines, which this build changes.

You need a saved **café**, a **bar** and a **restaurant** with a price level, and a **calendar
event** this week in the evening (say 7–8 pm Wednesday).

## Before you start

- [ ] The privacy page's Date night and family day paragraph says a date can be dinner, lunch,
  coffee, drinks or something to do, and that the AI gets **the times you are busy, never your
  events' titles** (published when the PR is merged).

## Plan a date

- [ ] Home → ❤️ Date night opens **Plan a date**. **Kind** row: Dinner · Lunch · Coffee · Drinks ·
  Something to do.
- [ ] **When**: seven chips, Today, Tomorrow, then the weekdays; 📅 **Pick a later day** for further
  out.
- [ ] Tap **Coffee**: the times change to 8:00 am · 10:00 am · 3:00 pm, 10:00 am picked. Plan it:
  **one café**, about an hour; the title says coffee, not "Date night".
- [ ] **Lunch** at 12:00 pm: a restaurant or café. **Drinks**: a bar. **Something to do**: a place
  to visit, then somewhere to eat if it fits.
- [ ] **Wednesday, Dinner, 7:00 pm** (your 7–8 pm event that day): the plan **does not show your
  event** as a stop; nothing is planned 7–8 pm; under the weather it says **"Planned around your
  calendar (busy 7:00 pm–8:00 pm)."**
- [ ] Turn **Fit around my calendar** off: the line under it says your calendar is not read; Plan
  it: no "Planned around" line, and the plan may use 7–8 pm.
- [ ] Family day: the same seven days and the calendar switch; your events are never stops.

## In the chat

- [ ] "Plan a coffee date Tuesday at 10": a card with a café at 10.
- [ ] "Plan a lunch date tomorrow": a card with lunch around noon.
- [ ] "Plan a date night Wednesday" (after "What's on Wednesday?" so Wilma has read the day): the
  card has no calendar event as a stop; Wilma says it planned around your calendar.

## When done

- [ ] Tell Claude what failed (with the step), or "all passed".
