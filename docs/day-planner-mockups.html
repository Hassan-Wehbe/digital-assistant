What you see

  • A short, timed list in Wilma's words: when to leave, not just when things start.
  • Anything that needs a decision (an overlap, a tight gap) comes first as "One thing to sort", with a one-tap fix.
  • Open my day opens the timeline (screen 2). Small questions ("when should I leave?") get a small answer.

Who works out what

  • Code works out drive times, leave-by times, rain chances and overlaps. Wilma never guesses a number.
  • Wilma picks what matters, writes it up and suggests fixes (the same pool, so take both).
2 · My day, the timeline · tap to try

Leave-by times, weather and overlaps in one list

‹Thursday, Oct 9Today ▾

Try it

  • Tap Take both on the overlap.
  • Tap ＋ Add, then Find a time for the dry cleaning, and pick an option. Changed rows get a green outline and keep the old time crossed out.

The rows

  • White cards are calendar events. Dashed cards are Wilma tasks.
  • Blue rows are drives: leave-by time, minutes with traffic, and the usual minutes when traffic adds a lot.
  • Rain rows show only when the chance is 50% or more at an event you drive to.
  • Free rows show gaps of 30 minutes or more, where tasks can go.

What it doesn't do

  • It never changes your calendar. The plan is a suggestion. Writing it back comes later, and only if you turn it on.
  • Video calls and events without a place get no drive row.

How tasks work

  • A task is a note of its own kind: what, how long, where, by when, and how important. It is kept and searchable like your other notes.
  • Wilma places today's tasks into free gaps or onto drives you're already making. If a task doesn't fit, it's listed as "not placed yet" and never squeezed in silently.
  • If you leave out how long it takes, Wilma estimates and says so ("about 20 min").
  • Later (D25): assign a task to another Wilma user, like your spouse, and it shows in their day.

Where should Tasks live? I'd add a Tasks tile on Home next to Vault, and also show them in My day.

Places on events

  • The event's address is looked up on the phone, the same way "Is this it?" works for places you share.
  • When only a name was found, Wilma says so and lets you check it, after the Craft & Common lesson.
  • Your answer is remembered for that event name ("Dentist" is Dr. Lee's office), so you're asked once.

Privacy

  • Event places and home are sent to Mapbox (drive times) and the Weather Service (forecast), only when you open or ask about your day. Event titles are not sent to them.
  • The privacy page and Play's Data safety form need your approval before this ships. That goes in the Play Console work we left for the end.
Questions for you

Before I write the plan

  • Buffer: 5 minutes to park and walk in, for every drive? (Or set per place, e.g. 10 at the pool.)
  • Rain: warn at 50% or more? Heat, storms and other weather alerts too?
  • Overlaps: besides "take both", should Wilma offer "ask someone else to drive" (useful once tasks can be assigned)?
  • Tasks: a Tasks tile on Home, as above?
  • Days: today only at first, then tomorrow? ("Plan tomorrow" the night before is useful for school mornings.)
  • Anything on these screens you'd change, add or drop.
